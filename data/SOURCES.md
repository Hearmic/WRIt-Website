## E. Landlord and Tenant Board applications — Tribunals Ontario open data

- Page: https://tribunalsontario.ca/en/about/open-data/ (dataset "LTB – Applications Received by e-File"; one .xlsx per quarter, links read from the page)
- Granularity found: quarterly (Ontario government fiscal quarters, April–March year); geography: Ontario (province-wide; no regional split in this dataset)
- Measure: total applications received, all filing methods (column 'total apps received - all filing methods'), rows L1, L2, T2, T6 and (from Jan–Mar 2022) Other
- Coverage: 2017-04-01 to 2026-06-30 (latest file: Q1 2026-27) · Retrieved: 2026-11-14
- Not parsed (kept in raw): 26 1 2017-01-01_2017-03-31 LTB Applications Received by e-File - 2016-2017.xlsx — multi-quarter or unrecognised layout; kept in raw only
- Data quirks: no file for Apr–Jun 2018 or Jul–Sep 2021; the Oct–Dec 2021 file only covers Oct 1–Dec 7, 2021; two older sheets have header dates that contradict their file names (file names used; each case is in the `note` field). 'Other' application types are only reported from Jan–Mar 2022 onward, so a total of all rows is not comparable over time. L1 is reported in every quarter. Numbers are subject to change (Tribunals Ontario note in every file).
- After Bill 60 (in force 2026-09-21): no post-change quarter is published yet. Shown on the site as pending.
- Raw: `data/raw/tribunals_open_data.htm`, `data/raw/ltb_efile/*.xlsx` · Processed: `ltb_applications.csv`, `ltb_applications.json`
- Used by: "Evictions for arrears: before and after Bill 60".

- Base: `https://ckan0.cf.opendata.inter.prod-toronto.ca/api/3/action/`
- Toronto Shelter System Flow: `datastore_search?resource_id=fb32967d-ee16-4d46-a880-0614c18f2137` (all records, limit 500 + offset). Kept population_group "All Population" and "Families". Latest month: 2026-10; dataset refreshed 2026-11-16 20:04:46.466977.
- Daily Shelter & Overnight Service Occupancy & Capacity: `package_show?id=daily-shelter-overnight-service-occupancy-capacity` → current-year datastore resource `42714176-4f05-44e6-b157-2b57f29b856a`; records for the latest OCCUPANCY_DATE (2026-11-18). Occupancy % = (sum OCCUPIED_BEDS + sum OCCUPIED_ROOMS) ÷ (sum CAPACITY_ACTUAL_BED + sum CAPACITY_ACTUAL_ROOM); beds and rooms are also reported separately.
- Retrieved: 2026-11-19 (re-fetched daily)
